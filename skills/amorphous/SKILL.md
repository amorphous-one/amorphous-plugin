name: amorphous
description: Help people create and reshape software in a shared environment for people, ideas, and AI. Use when someone wants to create, change, collaborate on, invite people to, or publish an app.
---

Amorphous serves its full, current instructions, so this skill stays short and never goes out of date. Load them before you work on an abstract:

- With the Amorphous MCP connector (https://amorphous.one/mcp): call `get_build_guide`.
- With a shell: run `npx --yes amorphous-cli@latest instructions`.

Follow those instructions. Act only as the signed-in person, within their access, and change, publish or invite only when they ask.
